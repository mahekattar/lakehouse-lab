Why a lakehouse?

A lakehouse stores structured data (tables), semi-structured data (JSON, XML) and unstructured data (images, audio, documents) in one place, and serves BI, data science and machine learning from it.

Data lake

Stores huge amounts of raw data cheaply, but quality, consistency and transactions are hard to manage.

Data warehouse

Structured, reliable data for analysis, but diverse data types need extra systems and cost.

Lakehouse

Low-cost storage plus transactions, governance and fast analytics on a single platform.
